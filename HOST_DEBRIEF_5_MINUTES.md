# Reality Check — Five-minute facilitator guide

**When to launch:** After the final winner has been announced. Click **Begin Reality Check** on the host screen. The competitive scores and resource economy are now frozen.

| Approximate time | Host action | Facilitator cue |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–1:15 | Begin Reality Check. Invite everyone to answer three prompts on their phones. | “Drop the venture-capitalist persona. Think as a mediator: what incentives did your business create?” |
| 1:15–2:00 | Reveal anonymous reflections. | Read out two contrasting answers. “Would the same mediator strategy work for both?” |
| 2:00–3:00 | Show the four-resource comparison matrix. | Emphasize that resource characteristics influence fragmentation, leverage and institutional control, but never determine outcomes alone. |
| 3:00–4:15 | Launch *The Peace Nobody Wants*. Participants select a first move and optionally give a rationale. Reveal the results. | “Whose economic interests does your first move touch, and who could be left outside the room?” |
| 4:15–5:00 | Conclude. Show the three lessons. | “A leader’s signature may not bind independent revenue holders; agreements must address incentives, legitimacy and implementation.” |

**Learning anchor:** Based on the session’s account of Kenny Gluck’s *Conflict and Resources* lecture. This is a facilitation heuristic, not a prediction model. The four resource economies are stylised for teaching.

**Use of participant answers:** The software displays them without names/team identifiers after Reveal. Responses are not moderated before display. In small rooms or when a player identifies themselves, content may still be recognizable; tell participants not to include identifying details. The host can advance before everyone submits.

**Optional follow-up question:** “If you had only one day for a mediation needs assessment, what evidence would tell you who really controls armed-group revenue?”
